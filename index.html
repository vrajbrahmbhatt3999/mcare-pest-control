What We Treat

Our Pest Control Services

Professional treatments for every pest problem — with warranty and contract options for lasting peace of mind.

Bed Bug Control

Spray treatment is carried out for bed bugs in two sessions. The first service eliminates the bed bugs completely, while the second service — done 15 to 20 days later — targets any eggs that may have hatched in the gap.

  • Commonly found in beds, sofas, mattresses, wooden chairs and furniture
  • Spray is applied to all required areas of the home
  • No need to vacate — chemical does not have a strong smell
  • After approximately 2 hours, the home can be cleaned and used normally

Warranty & Contract

  • 3-month warranty when contracted for two services
  • Full 1-year annual contract also available
  • Free complaint service if bed bugs return within the contract period

Termite Control

Drilling injection treatment is used for termite control. Pits are drilled approximately one foot apart where the wall meets the ground, and anti-termite chemicals are injected. The pits are then filled with white cement.

  • Two types of chemicals available — odorous and odorless
  • If odorous chemicals are used, keep the house closed for ~2 hours
  • Odorless treatment does not require the house to be closed

1-Year Contract — 3 Services Included:

01 Injection Treatment
02 Check-up Visit
03 Full-House Spray

1-Year Warranty

  • Full 1-year contract with 3 services
  • Re-treatment at no extra charge if termites return within contract period

Wood Borer Control

If you notice yellow or white powder falling from wooden furniture, it may indicate a wood borer infestation. Wood borers are wood-eating insects that can spread through wooden structures if left untreated.

  • Oil-based spray treatment applied to affected furniture and wood
  • House should be closed for approximately 2 hours after treatment (strong smell)
  • After 2 hours, the home can be cleaned and used normally

6-Month Warranty

  • 6-month warranty provided after service
  • Free complaint service if problem returns within the warranty period

Mosquito Control

Mosquitoes commonly breed near stagnant water, dumping grounds, or gutters. Professional spraying treatment provides effective mosquito control inside and around your home.

  • Spray treatment applied inside and around the property
  • House should be closed for approximately 2 hours after treatment
  • Effect lasts approximately 2–3 months
Prevention tip: Eliminate stagnant water sources around your home to reduce mosquito breeding.

Service Coverage

  • No specific warranty for this service
  • Spray effect lasts approximately 2–3 months

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your most common questions about our pest control services.

Which pest control services does M Care provide?

M Care Pest Control Services provides solutions for cockroaches, bed bugs, mosquitoes, wood borers, termites, lizards, ants, rats, fly control, and other general pest control requirements.

Do you provide pest control services in Vadodara?

Yes. M Care Pest Control Services is based in Vadodara and provides professional pest control services for homes and businesses in the area.

How many services are required for bed bug treatment?

Bed bug treatment requires two services. The first service eliminates the active bed bugs. The second service is done after approximately 15 to 20 days to destroy any eggs that may have hatched after the first treatment. A 3-month warranty is available when contracted for both services.

Do I need to leave the house during pest control treatment?

It depends on the service. For bed bug control with gel treatment, there is no strong smell and you do not need to vacate. For services such as fly control, lizard control, wood borer, and mosquito treatments, the house should be kept closed for approximately 2 hours after spraying. After that, it can be cleaned and used normally.

What is included in the termite annual contract?

The termite 1-year contract includes three services: the first service is a drilling injection treatment, the second is a check-up visit, and the third is a check-up visit with full-house spraying. If termites return within the contract period, the property can be treated again at no extra charge.

How long does mosquito treatment remain effective?

The effect of mosquito spray treatment lasts approximately 2 to 3 months. There is no specific warranty for this service, but regular treatment helps manage mosquito populations effectively.

What warranty is available for wood borer treatment?

A 6-month warranty is available for wood borer service. If the problem returns within the applicable warranty period, complaint service is provided free of cost according to the contract terms.

Do you provide complaint service during the contract period?

Yes. For services with a warranty or annual contract — such as bed bugs, termites, cockroach and ant control, wood borers, and rat control — complaint service is provided free of cost if the pest problem occurs again within the applicable contract period.

What is the cockroach and ant control annual contract?

The cockroach and ant 1-year contract consists of three services, with each service spaced approximately 4 months apart. A 4-month warranty is provided after each service. If cockroaches or ants appear during the contract period, complaint service is provided free of cost.

How do I know if my furniture is affected by wood borers?

A common sign of wood borer infestation is yellow or white powder falling from wooden furniture or structures. Wood borers are wood-eating insects that can spread through wooden items if left untreated. If you notice such powder, contact us for a professional assessment and treatment.
